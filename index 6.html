Questions, answered.

The stuff people actually ask before they buy a $19 bottle of oil.

What makes it "cold pressed"?+
The olives are pressed without added heat, which keeps more of the natural flavor and nutrients intact. It's the same standard that defines real extra virgin olive oil — we just don't cut corners on it.
Is the flavor real or added artificially?+
Real. Lemon zest, garlic, rosemary, basil, and tomato are infused directly into the oil during pressing — nothing is sprayed on or added as a flavoring agent afterward.
What sizes do you offer?+
Every flavor comes in a 250ml bottle for everyday cooking and a 500ml bottle if you go through oil fast or want fewer reorders.
How should I store it?+
Keep it in a cool, dark spot away from the stove's direct heat. The squeeze cap keeps air out between uses, so the flavor holds for months.
Do you ship outside the US?+
Not yet — we're starting local and expanding shipping zones as we grow. Join the list below and we'll let you know the moment your area opens up.
